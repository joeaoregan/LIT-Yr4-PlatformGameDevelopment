# Player Ship

The player commands an advanced starfighter equipped with dynamic thrusters and modular laser banks.

![Player Double Lasers](../images/screenshots/SpaceGame10Lasers.jpg)

---

## Ship Attributes

* **Speed**: High responsiveness with instant directional braking.
* **Health & Shields**: On-screen status bar tracking active hull integrity.
* **Weapons**:
  * **Standard Laser**: High-cadence single linear energy bolt.
  * **Double Laser**: Dual parallel high-damage plasma bolts.
* **Lives**: 3 initial lives with visual HUD icon tracking.
