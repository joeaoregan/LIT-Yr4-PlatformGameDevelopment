# Weapons & Power-ups

Defeating enemies yields collectible power-ups that enhance player combat capability.

![Laser Upgrades](../images/screenshots/SpaceGame10Lasers.jpg)

---

## Power-up Types

* **Double Laser**: Upgrades the ship's firing system to dual parallel beams, doubling damage output.
* **Shield Boost**: Instantly replenishes lost energy on the ship's hull status bar.
* **Extra Life**: Awards an additional player life icon on the HUD.
* **Score Multiplier**: Temporarily doubles point yields for destroyed hostile units.
